Frequently asked questions

How do I block apps on a schedule?

Tap Add schedule, choose the apps, the days and the time, and tap Save. The apps are blocked every week in that window, even when Offtide is closed.

Can different schedules block different apps?

Yes. Each schedule has its own apps, for example social media in the evening and games during work hours.

How do I block apps right now?

Choose apps under Manual block and tap Start manual block. They stay blocked until you tap Stop manual block.

Can a schedule run past midnight?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, such as 22:00–07:00, the block ends the next morning.

How do I change the order of my schedules?

Touch and hold a schedule, then drag it up or down.

Why can't I find an app in the list?

Apps are chosen in Apple's Screen Time picker, where they're grouped by category. Open a category, or use the search field at the top.

A schedule didn't start. What can I do?

Check that the schedule is switched on and has apps chosen. iOS allows a limited number of scheduled days in total, so if you have many schedules, try merging some. Make sure Offtide still has Screen Time access in Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access.

Does Offtide collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
